DIGEST THE FOLLOWING: 
- **同义词 (tóngyìcí):** Words with the exact same meaning.

- **近义词 (jìnyìcí):** Words with very close or similar meanings (most common).

- **反义词 (fǎnyìcí):** Antonyms (opposite words). [[1](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChineseLanguage/comments/o5luuo/does_anyone_know_of_a_good_chinese_thesaurus/), [2](https://preply.com/en/blog/chinese-synonyms-list/)]
- **帮助 (bāngzhù)** and **帮忙 (bāngmáng):** Both mean "to help," but _bāngzhù_ is more formal, while _bāngmáng_ is often used for specific, active tasks.

- **看 (kàn)** and **瞧 (qiáo):** Both mean "to look/see," but _kàn_ is standard and _qiáo_ is more colloquial/spoken. [[1](https://mandarinhouse.com/chinese-synonyms), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bp-Be1Qr7f4)]
